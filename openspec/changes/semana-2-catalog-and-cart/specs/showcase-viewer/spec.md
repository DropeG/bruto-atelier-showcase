## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Immersive Product Presentation
The system SHALL display product imagery in a way that respects the luxury, cinematic brand aesthetic. 
(MODIFIED: While previously the sole method of exploration was the full-viewport slider `ShowcaseViewer`, the new behavior shifts the primary catalog to the Editorial Grid. The full-viewport slider is now reserved strictly for Hero headers, cinematic project atmospheric views, and non-shopping experiences).

#### Scenario: Exploring a specific product category
- **WHEN** the user navigates to a broad shopping category (e.g., Mobiliario)
- **THEN** the immersive full-viewport slider plays at the top of the page as a Hero element, followed immediately below by the Editorial Grid.
